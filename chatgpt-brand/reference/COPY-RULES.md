# Copy and fact rules (non-negotiable)

Campaign GRID is a campaign workspace for one election seat, run by Katalys Media.
Audience: candidates, election agents, party organisations that fund campaigns. Tone: authoritative, checkable, no hype.

## What is real (you may claim this)
Six working jobs: candidate schedule (events + a queue of decisions), field deployment (teams by zone and polling district, tasks, map, "late" flag), content approval (fact-check must be closed before approval), media enquiries (logged with deadlines), expenses recorded in exact sen against the statutory cap (records facts, gives NO compliance advice, never blocks spending), incidents (triaged by urgency, closed with action taken).
Field team leads see only their own zone.
The wall: no link to citizen/office records (enforced by tests); no voting-intent field anywhere, no supporter estimates; on an office deployment the campaign area does not exist (404); compliance stays with the election agent.
Set up and trained by Katalys for the candidate and the election agent. Activation is by request, for an officially appointed candidate with a named election agent. It is NOT self-serve sign-up.
There is a live demo site (fictional candidate). Its public page is open; its team back end needs a login that Katalys gives on request.
