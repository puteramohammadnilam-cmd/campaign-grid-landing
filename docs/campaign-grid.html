THE DAILY WORK

The work moves.
The team stays aligned.

One place for the decisions, tasks and records a campaign handles every day.

Candidate schedule

Events and appearances, with a queue of decisions waiting on the candidate or election agent.

Field deployment

Teams and tasks by zone and polling district, with a map and a flag when work runs late.

Content approval

The fact-check must be closed before a draft can be approved.

Media enquiries

Journalist questions logged with their deadlines, so the team can see what needs a response.

Campaign expenses

Spending recorded in exact sen against the statutory cap. Records facts; does not give compliance advice or block spending.

Incidents

Problems triaged by urgency, then closed with a record of the action taken.

Field team leads see only their own zone.
